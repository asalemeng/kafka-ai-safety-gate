Nordhafen Bank AG - Board paper 14/2026 - STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL (invented example document)

Project Falcon is the planned acquisition of the retail lending portfolio of Elbe Kredit GmbH.
The board approved a maximum purchase price of 412 million euros in its meeting on 3 September.
Due diligence found that 6.8 percent of the acquired loans are more than ninety days overdue.
We expect to close the branch offices in Kiel and Rostock within eighteen months after signing.
About 240 employees will be affected and the works council has not been informed yet.
The announcement to the market is scheduled for the second week of January.
Until then the code name Falcon must not appear in any external e-mail or presentation.
Our fallback plan, if the regulator objects, is a joint venture with a 49 percent stake.
